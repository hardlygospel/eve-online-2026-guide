    Ships & ISK

    🚢 Ship Progression¶

    The Ship Size Ladder¶

    Rookie Ship (free)
    |
Frigate (T1) → Destroyer (T1)
    |               |
Cruiser (T1)    Interdictor / Command Destroyer
    |
Battlecruiser (T1) → Command Ship (T2)
    |
Battleship (T1)
    |
Capital Ships (Carrier, Dreadnought, Force Auxiliary, Supercarrier, Titan)

    Tech Levels Explained¶

    Tech Level Description Cost Range
    T1 Basic ships — cheap, accessible, good starting point 1M – 100M ISK
    T2 Specialised, powerful, require advanced skills 30M – 500M ISK
    Faction/Pirate Enhanced T1 stats, rare drops or LP purchases 50M – 1B+ ISK
    T3 Cruiser (Strategic) Modular, extremely powerful, can lose skills on death 500M – 2B+ ISK
    T3 Destroyer Flexible role destroyer 50M – 200M ISK
    Capital Only in null/low sec, massive power 5B – 100B+ ISK

    Recommended Progression Path¶

    Phase 1 — New Player (0–30 days) - Frigate → Destroyer → Cruiser - Stay in T1, learn your weapons and tank - Suggested: Caldari Drake path or Gallente Vexor path

    Phase 2 — Getting Comfortable (1–3 months) - T1 Battlecruiser (Drake, Hurricane, Prophecy, Myrmidon) - Start exploring T2 modules (they matter more than T2 ships) - Consider faction frigates for PvP (Dramiel, Worm, Garmur)

    Phase 3 — Serious ISK (3–6 months) - T1 Battleship for L4 missions - HAC (Heavy Assault Cruiser) for PvP - Consider Marauder or T3 Cruiser for endgame PvE

    Phase 4 — Endgame (6 months+) - Marauders (Golem, Paladin, Vargur, Kronos) for maximum L4/Abyss income - T3 Strategic Cruisers - Capital ships (requires corporation support)

    🔧 Fittings & Fitting Philosophy¶

    The Golden Rules of Fitting¶

    1. Fit for purpose — a mission ship and a PvP ship are completely different
    2. Never underfill CPU or Powergrid — every fitting should be tight
    3. T2 modules over faction for most slots — T2 is usually 80% of faction performance for 10% of the cost
    4. Always cap stable or have a plan for when cap runs out
    5. Overloading kills modules — use Thermodynamics skill and use it deliberately

    Module Slots Explained¶

    High Slots: Weapons, cloaks, utility (energy vampires/neutralisers, remote reps)

    Medium Slots: Shields, tackle (scramblers, webbers), cap boosters, sensor boosters, MWD/AB

    Low Slots: Armour, damage mods, speed mods, cap power relays

    Rig Slots: Permanent modifications (can't be removed without destroying them) — use wisely

    Key Module Types¶

    Module Purpose
    Afterburner (AB) Speed boost, doesn't increase signature
    Microwarpdrive (MWD) Massive speed, blooms signature (makes you easier to hit)
    Warp Scrambler Prevents target warping away (1 point)
    Warp Disruptor Longer range point (1 point)
    Stasis Webifier Slows target — critical for catching fast ships
    Sensor Dampener Reduces target lock range and scan resolution
    ECM Jams target — breaks lock randomly
    Energy Neutraliser Drains enemy capacitor
    Remote Shield/Armour Rep Repair allies in fleet
    Damage Control Massive bonus to all resistances — fit this always

    Fitting Tools¶

    • PyFA — the industry standard offline ship fitter. Simulates exact stats with your skills
    • EVE Fitting Tool (EFT) — older but still used
    • In-game fitting tool — use for quick checks

    ⛏️ Mining¶

    Mining is the backbone of EVE's economy. Every ship hull, module, and structure starts as ore.

    Types of Mining:

    • Asteroid Mining — basic ore in belts, low ISK but simple. Good starter income.
    • Ice Mining — slower, requires Ice Harvester modules, sells for consistent ISK (used for POS fuel and PLEX)
    • Moon Mining — corporation activity, requires moon drill structures, extremely lucrative
    • Gas Huffing — harvest gas in wormholes and null sec for T3 manufacturing

    Mining Ships: 1. Venture (frigate, free from career agents) — start here 2. Procurer / Retriever / Covetor (barges) 3. Skiff / Mackinaw / Hulk (exhumers, T2) 4. Orca (industrial command ship, boosts fleet) 5. Rorqual (capital, null sec only, enormous yield)

    Pro Tip: Never mine alone in low/null sec. Join a mining fleet with an Orca booster — you'll mine 40–60% faster.

    🎯 Mission Running¶

    Agents in stations give combat missions — kill specific NPCs, deliver items, or escort ships.

    Mission Levels: - L1 — tutorial level, 500K–2M ISK/hour - L2 — beginner, 5–15M ISK/hour - L3 — intermediate, 20–40M ISK/hour, cruiser/battlecruiser recommended - L4 — main endgame, 50–100M+ ISK/hour, battleship required - L5 — low-sec, extreme difficulty, 200M+ ISK/hour, rarely done solo

    L4 Mission Meta (2026): - Golem (Caldari Marauder) — highest efficiency, uses Bastion module to greatly boost tank and DPS - Paladin (Amarr Marauder) — laser variant, no ammo costs - Rattlesnake (Pirate Battleship) — drones + missiles, very beginner-friendly for L4 - Raven Navy Issue — strong budget option before Marauder skills

    Loyalty Points (LP): Every mission gives LP with the agent's faction. LP is converted to faction items via the LP store — this is where the real money is. Some LP stores pay 1,000–2,000 ISK per LP. Research which LP stores are most valuable before grinding.

    🔭 Exploration¶

    Exploration involves scanning down hidden sites with probes and running them for rewards.

    Site Types: - Relic sites — hack containers for modules, BPCs, ancient materials (most valuable) - Data sites — similar to relic, lower value but useful - Combat anomalies — rat-filled sites for ISK and escalations - Wormholes — unknown space with massive rewards

    Required Skills: - Astrometrics (scanning) - Survey (relic/data sites) - Hacking (data sites) - Archaeology (relic sites)

    Best Exploration Ships: - Heron (Caldari frigate) — starter, decent bonuses - Buzzard (Caldari Covert Ops) — best scanner, cloaking - Astero (Sisters of EVE frigate) — combat + exploration, extremely popular

    Exploration ISK: - High-sec relic: 5–20M per site - Low-sec relic: 20–80M per site - Null-sec relic: 50–200M per site - Wormhole relic: 50–500M+ per site

    Pro Tip: Use the Relic Analyser and Data Analyser with bonused ships. In null-sec, always D-Scan before warping to a site.

    ☄️ Ratting (PvE Combat in Null/Low Sec)¶

    Ratting means killing NPC pirates in asteroid belts and anomalies.

    Anomaly Ratting: Anomalies respawn constantly in null-sec. Clear them for bounty ISK.

    Best Ratting Ships (2026): - Ishtar (Gallente HAC) — sentry drone king, AFK-able, 80–120M ISK/hour - Vexor Navy Issue — budget version, still very effective - Rattlesnake — tank monster, great for newer players - Carrier (endgame) — 200–400M ISK/hour with fighters, requires null-sec corp

    Bounty System: Each NPC has a bounty. Killing it pays ISK directly to your wallet. Faction rats and officer spawns drop extremely rare modules worth billions.

    Escalations: Certain combat sites escalate into deadspace complexes worth 50–500M ISK. These can be sold to other players.

    🌀 Abyssal Deadspace¶

    Introduced in 2018, Abyssal Deadspace is instanced PvE in a dangerous pocket of space. You enter via a Triglavian filament, complete 3 rooms of combat, and escape through the exit — all within a 20-minute timer or your ship is destroyed.

    Tiers: - T1–T2: New player friendly, 10–30M per run - T3: Mid-level, 30–60M per run - T4: Serious income, 80–150M per run - T5: Highest tier, 150–300M+ per run, very dangerous

    Best Abyssal Ships (2026): - Gila (Guristas pirate cruiser) — the meta king for T3/T4 Abyss. Shields + drones, incredible tank - Cerberus — missile alternative - Sacrilege — armour, strong in certain filament types - Vagabond — speed-tanking specific weathers

    Filament Weather Types:

    Filament Effect
    Electrical Reduces shields, boosts armour
    Firestorm Reduces armour, boosts shields
    Exotic Reduces EM/Thermal resistance
    Gamma Reduces armour, boosts explosive
    Dark Reduces capacitor recharge

    Match your ship's strong resist to the filament type.

    📈 Station Trading¶

    Buy low, sell high. Pure market manipulation and arbitrage.

    How it works: 1. Find items with large bid/ask spreads 2. Place buy orders below current lowest sell 3. Wait for sellers to fill your order 4. Relist at competitive sell price for profit

    Popular Trading Hubs: - Jita 4-4 (The Forge) — the biggest market in the game, trillions of ISK daily - Amarr VIII — second largest - Dodixie — Gallente hub - Rens — Minmatar hub - Hek — smaller but less competition

    Required Skills: - Trade (more buy orders simultaneously) - Retail / Wholesale / Tycoon (more orders) - Broker Relations (reduce fees) - Accounting (reduce taxes)

    Income potential: Literally unlimited. Top traders make hundreds of billions per month.

    🌍 Planetary Interaction (PI)¶

    Extract resources from planets and process them into goods sold on the market or used in manufacturing.

    Setup: 1. Scan planets with your Planetary Interaction skill 2. Build Command Centres, Extractors, and Processors on planets 3. Set up routing between facilities 4. Collect and export finished goods every few days

    Best PI Products (2026): - Robotics — consistent high demand - Transcranial Microcontrollers — POCO taxes vary - Integrity Response Drones — used in structures

    Income: 20–100M ISK/day passively per account with good setups. Totally passive once configured.

    🔺 Pochven¶

    Pochven is a 27-system region created by the Triglavian Collective during the Triglavian Invasions. It exists as its own disconnected region accessible only via wormholes or filaments.

    Why Pochven: - Enormous bounties and unique loot - Triglavian standings required to operate safely (grind invasions to earn) - Some of the best ISK/hour in the game for prepared players - Unique implants and modules only found here

    Entry Methods: - Pochven filaments (cheap, available on market) - Natural wormhole connections - Standing with Triglavians (allows docking in Pochven stations)

    Income: 100–400M+ ISK/hour for skilled players with correct standings

    ⚡ Incursions¶

    Incursions are cooperative PvE events where the Sansha Nation invades high-sec systems. Players form fleets to fight them off for massive ISK rewards.

    Why Incursions are great: - Safe (high-sec) - 100–200M ISK/hour - Social — requires fleet coordination - Low skill requirement — bring a correctly fitted battleship

    Getting Started: Join the Warp to Me (WTM) or The Valhalla Project (TVP) incursion communities. They provide fleets, fits, and guidance for new incursion runners.

    Required: A T2-fit battleship matching their approved doctrines (usually Leshaks, Machariels, or Nightmare battleships)

    🏭 Industry & Manufacturing¶

    Buy blueprints, source minerals, manufacture ships and modules, sell for profit.

    Manufacturing Chain: 1. Research a Blueprint Original (BPO) to improve efficiency 2. Source minerals from mining or market 3. Manufacture using industrial facilities 4. Sell on market at a profit

    T2 Manufacturing: More complex — requires invention (a random process turning T1 BPCs into T2 BPCs). Much more profitable but more involved.

    Income: Varies wildly based on market conditions. Skilled industrialists make billions consistently.
