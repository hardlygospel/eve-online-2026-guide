

## 📅 Progression Guide

<div align="center" markdown="1">
<img src="https://images.evetech.net/types/32880/render?size=256" alt="The Venture — every legend started with one ship" width="280" />
</div>



### Week 1 — The Foundation

1. **Complete all 5 career agent mission chains** — free ships, ISK, skills
2. **Train the Magic 14 to level 3** — don't rush, queue is passive
3. **Do the Sisters of EVE epic arc** ("The Blood-Stained Stars") — 40+ missions, great ISK and standings, unlocks better agents
4. **Explore the market** — understand supply/demand basics
5. **Join a new player friendly corporation** — EVE is better with others

### Month 1 — First Real ISK

1. Train to fly a **Drake or Vexor** (your race's best early PvE ship)
2. **Run L2 then L3 missions** for steady ISK
3. Set up **basic PI on 2–3 planets** — passive income running in background
4. **Learn to scan** — even basic exploration pays well
5. Save 50–100M ISK as a reserve — you will lose ships

### Month 2–3 — Choosing Your Path

**Path A: Mission Runner**
- Train to T1 Battleship → L4 missions
- Research best LP stores for your faction
- Target 50–80M ISK/hour

**Path B: Drone Farmer**
- Train Vexor Navy Issue / Ishtar
- Join a null-sec corporation
- Rat anomalies for 80–150M ISK/hour

**Path C: Trader**
- Train Trade skills
- Start with 50–100M ISK capital
- Focus on Jita margins

**Path D: Explorer**
- Train Covert Ops ship
- Head to null-sec or wormholes
- Scan and hack for variable but excellent ISK

**Path E: PvP Fighter**
- Join Faction Warfare or a PvP-focused corporation
- Fly cheap frigates, die a lot, learn constantly
- Move to HACs and T3 cruisers as skills allow

### Month 4–6 — Serious Wealth

- First **Marauder** or **T3 Cruiser** — your earning power triples
- Start **manufacturing** or **PI** at scale
- Consider **joining a null-sec alliance** for ratting in carriers
- Begin accumulating wealth for **PLEX** (paying subscription with ISK)

### 1 Year+ — The 1%

- Carrier/Dreadnought capable
- Multiple income streams running simultaneously
- Billions in reserve
- Possibly running your own operation, corporation, or trading empire

## 🖥️ Understanding the UI & HUD

<div align="center" markdown="1">
<img src="https://images.evetech.net/types/587/render?size=256" alt="The Rifter — learn your HUD here" width="280" />
</div>



EVE's interface is complex but completely customisable. Spend time on your UI setup — it pays dividends for thousands of hours.

### Essential HUD Elements

#### Overview
The overview is your radar — it shows everything around you. **Configure it immediately.**

1. Right-click the Overview tab → **Open Overview Settings**
2. Create multiple tabs:
   - **PvE** — show NPCs, structures, gates, stations, wrecks
   - **PvP** — show all players, drones, missiles
   - **Travel** — show only gates and stations
   - **Mining** — show asteroids, gas clouds, ice fields

**Recommended Overview Columns (in order):**
Icon | Name | Distance | Velocity | Transversal | Angular | Corp | Alliance | Tag

#### Bracket Settings
`Alt+Shift+B` toggles brackets. In busy systems, turn off excess brackets to reduce visual noise.

#### D-Scan (Directional Scanner)
`V` — the most important button in EVE. D-Scan shows everything within 14.3 AU in your chosen direction. Use it constantly.

- Set range to max (14.3 AU) for general awareness
- Narrow the angle to 5° to pinpoint ships at specific structures
- Scan every 3–5 seconds in dangerous space

#### Local Chat
The Local channel shows every player in your current system. **Watch it constantly.** Neutrals and reds appearing in local = danger. In wormhole space, there is no local — you don't know who is with you.

#### Probe Scanner
`Alt+P` — opens the scanning interface for exploration sites and cosmic signatures.

#### Ship HUD
Your ship's capacitor, shields, armour, and hull are shown on the HUD circle. Learn to read it at a glance — your cap dropping means you're in trouble.

### Recommended UI Layout

```
+---------------+-----------------------------+-----------+
|   Chat/Local  |        Main View            | Overview  |
|               |                             |           |
|               |                             |           |
|               |                             |           |
+---------------+-------------+---------------+-----------+
|  Selected Item / Info       |   Inventory / Fitting     |
+-----------------------------+---------------------------+
```

- **Overview** — top right, always visible
- **Local** — bottom left
- **Selected Item** — bottom centre-left
- **Inventory** — bottom right (collapsed when not needed)
- **D-Scan** — learn to open/close quickly with `V`

### HUD Customisation Pro Tips

- **Resize the HUD ring** — right-click the HUD → resize. Smaller frees screen space for the overview
- **Lock windows** — right-click window title → lock. Prevents accidental closing in combat
- **Transparency sliders** — under Escape → Display & Graphics → Window Opacity. Raise it so the background is more visible
- **Overview presets** — download community presets from EVE forums. Don't build your first overview from scratch
- **Camera settings** — set "Track Selected" on but reduce "Camera Shake" to zero. Shake is disorienting in fleet fights
- **UI Scale** — if on 4K, scale the UI up under Display settings
- **Compact mode** on most windows saves significant screen real estate in combat

## 📚 Skills & Skill Planning

<div align="center" markdown="1">
<img src="https://images.evetech.net/types/29984/render?size=256" alt="The Tengu — the reward for months of training" width="280" />
</div>



### How Skills Work

EVE skills train in real time — even when you're offline. Each skill has 5 levels, and each level takes longer than the last. Level 5 of a skill can take weeks. Skills are permanent once trained.

### Attributes & Implants

Skills have two associated attributes (e.g., Perception + Willpower for combat skills). Higher attributes = faster training.

- **Implants** — plug into your head and boost attributes or give specific bonuses. Lost when your pod is destroyed.
- **Learning implants (slots 1–5)** — boost training speed. Worth using in high-sec where your pod is safe.
- **Standard implant set for new players:** +3 to all five attributes (~5% training speed boost)
- **Slot 6–10 implants** — specific combat/industry bonuses (e.g., +5% shield HP, +3% weapon damage)

### Skill Priority for All New Players

**Week 1 — Survive and earn**
- Learning skills (Cybernetics to 3 for implants)
- Navigation (Afterburner, Microwarpdrive to 4)
- Shield/Armour basics to 3
- Your race's weapon system to 4
- Spaceship Command to 4

**Month 1 — Efficiency**
- All core skills (the "Magic 14") to 4
- Mining skills if you plan to mine
- Trade skills if you plan to trade
- Electronic systems basics

### The Magic 14 — Core Skills Every Pilot Needs

These 14 skills benefit every single playstyle. Train them all to at least level 4:

1. Energy Grid Upgrades
2. Engineering
3. Shields (Shield Management, Shield Operation)
4. Mechanics
5. Hull Upgrades
6. Navigation
7. Afterburner
8. Evasive Maneuvering
9. Warp Drive Operation
10. Electronics Upgrades
11. CPU Management
12. Power Grid Management
13. Thermodynamics (overloading)
14. Capacitor Management / Capacitor Systems Operation

### Skill Planning Tools

- **EVEmon** — desktop skill planner, shows training time for any plan
- **PyFA** — ship fitting tool that shows exact skill requirements
- **EVE Skillboard** — share your skills publicly
- **Omega Queue** — plan years of skills in advance

### Remap Strategy

Character attributes can be remapped once per year (plus one free remap for new accounts). Before a major skill training block, remap your attributes to match the primary/secondary attributes of those skills. A well-planned remap can save weeks of training time.
